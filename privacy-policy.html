🛡️ Official Privacy Statement

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective for all Selected Sports Web & Mobile Platform Users

Welcome to Selected Sports ("we", "our", or "us"), available at https://selectedsports.in. We are fully committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring your personal and athletic data is treated with utmost care, transparency, and top-tier security standards. This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, how it is utilized, and the rights you maintain over your data across our web application and mobile experiences.

1 Information We Collect

When you register, schedule matches, or participate in cricket fixtures on the Selected Sports platform, we collect information required to operate sports fixtures, team rosters, and player profiles:

  • Personal & Identity Information: Full name, verified mobile/WhatsApp number, city, date of birth, jersey number, and jersey size.
  • Player Profile Photograph: Optional digital player photographs uploaded and cropped by you for your official digital player ID card, match scorecard displays, and team lineups.
  • Cricket & Performance Information: Playing role (Batsman, Bowler, All-rounder, Wicketkeeper), batting/bowling styles, match availability, runs, wickets, tournament statistics, and awards.
  • Tournament & Auction Data: Franchise team rosters, points, base prices, player draft entries, and match scheduling records.

2 Device & Application Permissions

The Selected Sports web and mobile app may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Accessed strictly when you choose to take or upload a profile picture or team badge. We never scan, browse, or read your photo gallery without explicit action.
  • Internet & Network State: Essential for synchronizing live match squads, player availability statuses, scorecards, and live team fixtures with our secure cloud servers.

3 How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate sports management and operational needs:

  • To authenticate and manage your player, organizer, or admin account safely.
  • To schedule turf fixtures, coordinate team rosters, and track confirmed player attendance.
  • To generate verified digital player ID cards, public scorecard summaries, and city rankings.
  • To send match notifications, confirmation updates, and timing alerts via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To prevent fraudulent registrations, duplicate accounts, and maintain fair play across matches.
🔒 Zero Advertising Data Selling

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, phone numbers, or match history to third-party advertising networks or commercial data brokers.

4 Data Storage & Security Standards

All user information and match data are securely housed in Supabase (PostgreSQL) enterprise infrastructure equipped with:

  • End-to-End SSL/TLS Encryption: All communications between your device and our servers are encrypted using modern cryptographic protocols.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Database-level policies ensure organizers, players, and viewers only access information permitted by their specific roles.
  • Strict Session Protections: PINs and authentication tokens are kept safe and never stored as plaintext in unauthorized storage.

5 Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with industry-standard, compliant infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Cloud database, storage, and authentication provider.
  • Google Maps Platform: For cricket venue mapping, turf coordinates, and ground discovery in Pune and surrounding regions.

6 Account & Data Deletion Rights

You have full ownership and rights over your personal data. At any time, you may:

  • Review and update your profile details (name, city, jersey number, profile photo) directly in your Profile tab.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account, player records, profile images, and phone registration.

To request permanent account deletion, email our administrative team at selectedsportsindia@gmail.com with your registered mobile number. Complete purge requests are processed within 7 business days.

7 Children's Privacy

Our platform facilitates community and competitive cricket. For youth tournaments or Under-19 leagues, registration should be conducted with the guidance and consent of a parent, guardian, or official academy coach.

8 Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new platform features, tournament rules, or regulatory guidelines. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated revision date.
